Why we built it

Most courses stop where the real job begins

We kept meeting capable people who'd finished a Java course and still couldn't ship anything, because tutorials end at the language, and the actual work is everything after it.

The gap we set out to close

Watching video after video teaches you to recognize Java, not write it. And even a great language course leaves you at the starting line of a real job: no Git, no databases, no Spring, no deployment, no idea how senior engineers actually think.

So we built the path we wished existed, one continuous line from "what is a variable" all the way to your own application running on the public internet, with every step you actually do yourself.

Built by engineers who learned this the hard way, so you don't have to.

The domains

Twelve domains, each a belt of its own

Pick a domain and earn its belt white → black; every domain has a dan track of advanced topics beyond black belt. Start at the very beginning or jump to what you need.

  1. ☕ Java & the JVM: fundamentals, generics, exceptions, regex, modern Java, concurrency, the JVM & performance.
  2. 🧠 Computer Science & Algorithms: data structures, dynamic programming, advanced algorithms.
  3. 🌐 Web, APIs & Frameworks: web development, REST APIs, Spring Boot.
  4. 🗄️ Data & Persistence, databases & SQL.   🔌 Systems & Networking, sockets & protocols.
  5. 🔐 Security & Cryptography: hashing, AES-GCM, signatures, the JCA.
  6. 🛂 Identity & Access (IAM): authn vs authz, OAuth 2.0 & OIDC (all flows), SAML & web SSO, service-to-service & SPIFFE, PKI & certificates, JWT/JOSE.
  7. 🚀 DevOps & Delivery: build tools, Git, Docker, CI/CD, deployment.
  8. 🏛️ Architecture & Design,  ⛩️ Senior Track (Dan), system design, distributed systems, real-world code.
  9. 🏆 Coding Challenges & 🛠️ Real-World Projects, interview-style tournaments and four apps you deploy to the internet.

Our story

Why we built DevDojo

Learning to code online is broken in a specific way

Tutorials teach you syntax. Video courses teach you to follow along. Then both end, and you're alone in the gap between "I finished the course" and "I can build and ship real software." That gap has a name, tutorial hell, and it's where most people quietly give up.

DevDojo exists to close that entire gap in one place. We built the course we wished existed: one that starts by explaining what a program even is, makes you type from the first minute, refuses to hide the unglamorous professional skills, and doesn't declare victory until your code is running on the public internet.

— built by engineers who learned the hard way, so you don't have to
