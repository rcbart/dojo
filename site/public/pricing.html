FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. The entire belt path — foundations through the four deploy-to-web projects — is free while JavaDojo is in training. No card required, and your progress saves to your account.

Why a one-time price instead of a subscription?

The dojo is a finite, structured curriculum, not an endless feed — so a single lifetime purchase fits how it's actually used, and there's no recurring charge to forget about. Future content is included.

What exactly does Black Belt unlock?

The senior Dan Track (system design, failure-first distributed systems, and working with real code — drawn from real interviews at top companies), every tournament, AI-verified exercise runs, and shareable completion certificates.

Can I expense this or buy for my team?

Yes — the Team plan gives every seat full access plus a dashboard to assign streams and track completion, with centralized billing. Volume pricing starts at 5 seats.

What if it's not for me?

Any paid plan comes with a 30-day refund, provided you've completed less than a quarter of the Dan Track — plenty of room to explore whether it's right for you, while keeping the guarantee fair for everyone. One refund per customer. If you've genuinely worked through most of the senior track in a month, you've had the value; before that point, we'll happily refund you, no fuss.
